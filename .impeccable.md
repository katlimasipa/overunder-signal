## Design Context

### Users

OverUnderAuto is for a broad audience using phones, tablets, and desktop computers. The interface should make a digit-based trading strategy and its risk limits straightforward to inspect and configure.

### Brand Personality

Premium, efficient, and easygoing. Keep the writing clear, measured, and practical; avoid emojis, novelty symbols, and distracting visual flourishes.

### Aesthetic Direction

Build a polished trading workspace inspired by the supplied bot dashboards: compact controls, legible market data, clear session status, and a disciplined mix of white and blue with a restrained complementary accent. Offer a deliberate dark theme as well as the light theme. Avoid decorative illustrations and anything that could be mistaken for a live market connection.

### Design Principles

- Put the strategy controls and market readout in a clear, scannable workspace.
- Make risk limits and recovery multipliers easy to find and adjust.
- Keep navigation, dropdowns, and form controls visually consistent.
- Adapt the same core tasks to touch and pointer input without hiding controls.
- Treat account and trading connections as previews until explicitly integrated.
